About me.

I'm an ECE student who gets disproportionately excited when an idea goes from “wait, could this work?” to an actual working thing.

I'm especially drawn to robotics, aerospace, electronics, computer vision and the physics behind the things we build. I like learning by making — which usually means a suspicious number of wires, diagrams, simulations and tabs open at once.

Long term, I'd love to work at the intersection of engineering and fundamental science, while continuing to build things simply because they're fascinating.
